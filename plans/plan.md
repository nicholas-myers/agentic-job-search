# 30-Day Job + Income Plan

## Objective
Secure a software engineering role within 30 days while generating bridge income to support your family.

## Core Strategy
- Track A: Land a full-time SWE role quickly.
- Track B: Start immediate income streams (contract, freelance, tutoring, automation gigs).

---

## Day 0-1: Stabilize + Prepare
- File unemployment and review severance/benefits.
- Build a 30-day budget and cut non-essential expenses.
- Update:
  - Resume (base version)
  - LinkedIn headline + About
  - Master achievements doc (projects, metrics, impact)
- Choose top 2-3 target roles:
  - Example: Senior Backend Engineer, Full-Stack Engineer, Platform Engineer
- Send initial outreach to 20 trusted contacts.

---

## Week 1 (Days 1-7): Build Pipeline
- Create 2 tailored resume variants.
- Apply to 8-12 quality roles/day (referrals first).
- Send 10 outreach messages/day (ex-colleagues, managers, recruiters).
- Connect with 30 recruiters in your stack.
- Do 60-90 min/day interview prep.
- Clean up GitHub/LinkedIn for credibility.

### Week 1 Targets
- 50-70 applications
- 40-60 outreach messages
- 3-8 screens booked

---

## Week 2 (Days 8-14): Increase Interview Conversion
- Prioritize warm leads and referrals over cold applies.
- Complete 3 mock interviews.
- Prepare 8-10 STAR stories.
- Follow up on all applications after 5-7 days.
- Define compensation floor and non-negotiables.

### Week 2 Targets
- 8-15 active interviews/screenings
- Clear signal on best-converting role profile

---

## Week 3 (Days 15-21): Optimize + Focus
- Review funnel data and drop low-converting channels.
- Narrow to strongest job narrative.
- Focus prep on patterns showing up in real interviews.
- Prep references (3 people ready to respond quickly).

### Week 3 Targets
- Consistent interview performance
- Final-round interviews in motion

---

## Week 4 (Days 22-30): Offer Push + Backup Income
- Keep applying while interviewing (do not pause pipeline).
- Compare opportunities on:
  - Total compensation
  - Stability
  - Remote/flexibility
  - Healthcare/parental benefits
- Continue bridge income until offer is signed/start date confirmed.

### Week 4 Targets
- 1+ offer or deep-stage pipeline with income bridge running

---

## Bridge Income Plan (Start This Week)
Prioritize the fastest path to paid work using SWE skills:
- Freelance/contract development
- 2-week fixed-scope consulting sprints
- Interview coaching/coding tutoring
- Small business automation (Zapier/Make/scripts)
- Part-time support engineering or QA automation roles

### Simple Market Offer
"I help teams ship backend/API features and automate manual workflows in 2-week fixed-scope sprints."

---

## Daily Operating Schedule (Mon-Fri)
- 8:00-10:00: Applications (tailored, referral-first)
- 10:00-11:00: Outreach + follow-ups
- 11:00-12:00: Interview prep
- 1:00-3:00: Freelance prospecting / paid work
- 3:00-4:00: Mock interview or portfolio/project polish
- 4:00-4:30: Metrics review + next-day planning

---

## Daily Metrics Dashboard
Track each day:
- Applications sent
- Outreach messages sent
- Replies received
- Screens booked
- Interviews completed
- Revenue earned (bridge income)

---

## Contingency Trigger (If Low Traction by Day 10)
- Rewrite resume headline + top bullets with stronger metrics.
- Narrow focus to one target role.
- Shift effort to referrals and direct intros.
- Increase applications to smaller firms + contract-to-hire.
- Add "Open to contract / immediate start" across profiles.
